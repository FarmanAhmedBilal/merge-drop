MERGE DROP ARCADE — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Merge Drop Arcade is a free collection of browser games. This policy explains what information the games handle and how sponsor links work.

Information we collect

The arcade has no accounts or analytics and does not transmit gameplay records or personal information. It displays locally configured sponsor banners; those banners do not profile players. If a third-party ad network is added in the future, this policy will be updated to describe its data practices before it is enabled.

Information stored on your device

The games save best scores, games played, and sound preference using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent to us, and is erased if you clear your browser data or use the game's data reset.

Sponsor links

Sponsor banners may link to third-party websites. Following a link takes you outside the arcade, where that site's own privacy policy applies. We do not receive information about your visits to those sites.

Permissions

The game requests no permissions and does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Children

The games contain no mature content and are designed for general audiences. Any future third-party advertising will be reviewed for age suitability and reflected in this policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact listed on the app's store page.
