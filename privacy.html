MERGE DROP — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Merge Drop is a free puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game handles — which is, in short, almost none.

Information we collect

We collect no information. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not transmit any data off your device.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, games played and sound preference using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and is erased if you clear your browser data, delete the app, or use the game's own data reset.

Permissions

The game requests no permissions and does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.

Children

The game contains no mature content and is suitable for all ages. Because nothing is collected, it complies with children's privacy laws.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact listed on the app's store page.
